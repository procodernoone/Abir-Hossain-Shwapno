About

sec.01

Hey, I'm Abir Hossain Shwapno, a 16-year-old maker from Bangladesh. I code, build robots, sketch graphics, make websites and get way too excited about science projects.

I started with Python, fell into web dev, and now I'm deep in robotics, AI, and design. My projects include an ESP32 rover controlled by hand gestures from a Flutter app, a browser hand-pinch servo controller, the Astro Rover, and a Godot platformer for Android.

I also love teaching, tinkering, and drinking chai while debugging.
